== Schedule Upload

=== Team/Division IDs

. Click on HQ
. Click Competition => Schedule Upload => Team/Division IDs
. Filter to the desired Season and Subseason
. Manually map Team Names to Team IDs and Division Names to Division IDs
+
[NOTE]
====
Key is YYDUPT

* YY is always year start (ie 25 for all seaons in 2025-2026 year - including winter and spring leagues)
* D is day of the week (S for Sunday, M for Monday, T for Tuesday, W for Wednesday, H for Thursday, F for Friday, A for Saturday)
* U is sub season (F for Fall, W for Winter, S for Spring) For Tuesday I use 1 or 2 for first or second half of the season
* P is Pool for Tuesday leagues (A for Pool A, B for Pool B)
* T is Team (ie 1, 2, 3, etc.)
====
image::step_6.png[]
. Click `SAVE TEAM/DIVISION IDS` button

=== Create Upload File

. Change the CSV file created to have the following headers to match SportsEngine's required format:
+
[%autowidth]
|===
| Format | Start_Date | Start_Time | End_Date | End_Time | Location | Team1_ID | Team2_ID | Description | Location_Url | Location_Details | All_Day_Event | Event_Type | Team1_Is_Home
|===
. Change the team names to the corresponding Team IDs from the previous step
. Save the file as a CSV

=== Upload Schedule

. Click on HQ
. Click Competition => Schedule Upload => Schedule Upload
. Click `Choose File`
. Leave Schedule Format as `SportsEngine File Format`
. Upload the CSV file created in the previous step by clicking `IMPORT SCHEDULE` button
