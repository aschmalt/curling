== Purpose

This document defines the standard Evergreen Curling Club process for creating and managing league sessions within SportsEngine.

The goals of this guide are:

* Ensure all league coordinators use a consistent workflow.
* Protect historical rosters, schedules, standings, and team records.
* Provide a repeatable process for creating new seasons.
* Ensure all registered participants are properly rostered.
* Establish a standardized schedule upload process.
* Serve as training material for new league coordinators.

This guide covers:

* Registration verification
* League configuration
* Season creation
* Subseason creation
* Team creation
* Team copy considerations
* Rostering
* Roster reconciliation
* Schedule uploads
* Validation and publishing

== Quick Start Checklist

Before beginning, ensure the following tasks are completed:

* [ ] Registration verified
* [ ] Registration roster exported
* [ ] League verified
* [ ] Season created
* [ ] Fall subseason created
* [ ] Teams created
* [ ] Registrants rostered
* [ ] Roster audit completed
* [ ] Schedule uploaded
* [ ] Schedule validated
* [ ] Public pages verified

== Evergreen Policy

[IMPORTANT]
====
The current registration is the official source of truth for league participants.

League coordinators must roster participants from the current registration.

Do not use prior-year rosters as the source of truth for the current season.
====

[IMPORTANT]
====
Historical teams, rosters, schedules, standings, and game results are considered permanent records.

League setup activities should never modify historical records.
====

== Safety Rules

[WARNING]
====
DO NOT MODIFY HISTORICAL TEAMS.

A team associated with a previous season or subseason is a historical record.

Historical teams may be referenced by:

* Published schedules
* Team pages
* Historical standings
* Historical rosters
* Public league archives

Editing a historical team may affect previous seasons.
====
 
[WARNING]
====
DO NOT CLEAR THE ROSTER OF A HISTORICAL TEAM.

If you are unsure whether a team belongs to a prior season, stop immediately and verify before making changes.
====

[WARNING]
====
DO NOT USE TEAM COPY AS THE STANDARD WORKFLOW.

Until Evergreen verifies exactly how SportsEngine handles team copying and roster inheritance, the preferred and supported workflow is:

* Create a new season
* Create a new subseason
* Create new teams
* Roster current registrants onto those teams

This approach is the safest and least likely to affect historical records.
====

[WARNING]
====
STOP IMMEDIATELY IF:

* A previous-season roster changes unexpectedly.
* A copied team appears to update a historical team.
* Historical schedules disappear.
* Historical standings change.
* A schedule upload affects another season.

Contact a SportsEngine administrator before proceeding.
====

== League Setup Overview

The standard workflow is:

. Verify Registration
. Create League Season
. Create Fall Subseason
. Create Teams
. Roster Players
. Perform Roster Reconciliation
. Prepare Schedule
. Upload Schedule
. Validate League
. Publish

== Step 1: Verify Registration

Navigate to:

----
Registration
  -> Registrations
----

Locate the registration for the league being configured.

Example:

----
League 06 - Thursday Evening - Fall 2026
----

* Registration status

* Waitlist count

Active Count: 52

== Step 6: Create Teams

=== Standard Evergreen Method

The preferred Evergreen workflow is to create NEW teams every season.

Navigate to:

----
Competition
  -> Sport Management
  -> Teams
----

Select:

* League
* Season
* Subseason

.Teams Tab
image::05-teams.png[Teams Tab]

Example:

----
League: Thursday Night
Season: 2026-2027
Subseason: Fall
----

=== Create Team

For each team:

. Click Add Team.
. Enter Team Name.
. Enter Short Name.
. Enter Abbreviation.
. Verify League.
. Verify Season.
. Verify Subseason.
. Save.

Repeat for all teams.

=== Team Creation Verification

After creating each team verify:

* Team belongs to Fall 2026.
* Team belongs to 2026-2027.
* Team belongs to correct league.
* Team roster count is zero.
* Team is not associated with previous seasons.

=== Team Naming Best Practices

Use actual team names whenever possible.

Examples:

----
Schmaltz
Tomlinson
Lilyhammer
Plaid
----

Avoid:

----
Team1
Team2
Team3
----

unless official team names are not yet known.

== Team Copy Procedure (Optional)

[WARNING]
====
Team copy is OPTIONAL and not recommended until SportsEngine behavior has been verified.
====

Before using team copy:

Verify all of the following:

* Copy creates a new Team ID.
* Historical team remains unchanged.
* Historical roster remains unchanged.
* Historical standings remain unchanged.
* Historical schedule remains unchanged.

=== Team Copy Validation Process

Test only one team.

. Copy one team.
. Open copied team.
. Confirm unique Team ID.
. Confirm historical team remains unchanged.
. Determine roster behavior.

If old players are copied:

. Verify copied team is independent.
. Remove inherited players ONLY from copied team.
. Verify historical roster remains unchanged.

If historical roster changes:

STOP IMMEDIATELY.

Use newly created teams instead.

== Step 8: Registration-to-Roster Reconciliation

Matching counts are NOT sufficient.

Example:

----
52 Registrants
52 Player Slots Filled
----

This does not guarantee correctness.

One player may have been assigned twice while another is missing.

=== Required Validation

Verify:

----
Active Registrants            52
Rostered Participants         52
Unassigned Participants        0
Duplicate Assignments          0
----

Replace 52 with actual registration count.

=== Name-by-Name Verification

Compare registration export against rosters.

Verify:

* No missing participants.
* No duplicate participants.
* No inactive participants.
* No waitlisted participants.
* No participants from a previous year.

=== Team Audit

For every team verify:

* Team size correct
* Team members correct
* Skip correct
* Vice correct

=== Initial Import Settings

Recommended settings:

* Enable Commenting on Games = Yes
* Replace All Previously Uploaded Events = No

[WARNING]
====
Do NOT check "Replace All Previously Uploaded Events" during the first upload.

Validate the schedule first.

Use replacement only when you understand exactly what events will be affected.
====

* Sheets correct
* Teams correct
